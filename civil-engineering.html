Civil Engineering & Construction

Civil Merc designs and builds the works a district uses every day: the road under the tyres, the bridge that keeps a community connected, the drain that still carries water after the first heavy rain. In Abuja this work is judged by how it holds, not by how it looks on opening day.

What the service covers

The civil scope is roads and highways, bridges and flyovers, water supply, drainage, and wastewater. Structural safety sits in the same brief. A highway job is alignment, pavement, drainage, and how the road meets what is already there. A bridge or flyover is foundations, deck, and the approaches people actually drive. Water supply, drainage, and wastewater are sized for the catchment and for how Abuja rains, not for a dry-season sketch.

How we build in Abuja

Karshi, the airport corridor, and the inner districts do not drain the same way, and they do not carry the same traffic. We check alignment, hydrology, and structure before plant moves. Designs follow Nigerian codes, and where the specification calls for them, Eurocodes and ASTM. BIM and 3D modelling put the road, the structure, and the services in one model so clashes are found before concrete is poured.

On site, materials and workmanship are held to that specification. Quality assurance is part of the daily work, not a visit at the end. There is zero tolerance on accidents. Handover is aimed at zero defects: the road, the crossing, and the drains are walked and checked before they are handed over.

What you receive

  • A design that has been checked for alignment, water, and structure before construction.
  • Work built to Nigerian codes, with Eurocodes and ASTM where the brief requires them.
  • Quality assurance on site and a finish prepared for zero-defect handover.
  • Infrastructure that is simpler to maintain, because drainage and structure were sized for the place.

Gidan Daya, Karshi

Gidan Daya Bridge, Culvert and Road in Karshi, Abuja, is the kind of job this service is for. The bridge is the crossing. The culvert takes the water that would otherwise sit on the road or cut it. The road ties both into the neighbourhood they serve. None of the three works on its own: a bridge without a road does not move people, and a road without a culvert fails at the first storm. The project is in Abuja, and it is built as one piece of infrastructure.

From drawing to opening

If you have a road, a crossing, a flyover, or a water and drainage scheme, bring the brief to Suite E3, SHM Complex, before the Federal Ministry of Works, Mabushi. We take it from the survey and the model through to handover. Quality you can see, reliability you can feel.
