Why us?

A proven record, shaped around every client

Civil Merc Ltd is a civil engineering and real estate development company. We deliver residential, commercial, and infrastructure work with a focus on reliability, precision, and safety.

Clients choose us for a track record of more than 23 completed projects, a customised solution for every brief, and sustainable practice that reduces the carbon footprint of each job. We use BIM, 3D modelling, and advanced construction techniques, and we hold a zero-tolerance policy on accidents.
