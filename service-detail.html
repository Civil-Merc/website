Services

Real Estate Development

Civil Merc develops property people can live in, work in, and hold. In Abuja that starts with the plot: access, drainage, what the district already supports, and what a buyer or tenant will actually pay for. We do not set a column until that reading is clear.

What the service covers

Residential work includes apartments, gated communities, and homes sized for how families in the city live. Commercial work covers offices, retail, and mixed-use schemes that have to earn their place on the street. Where the opportunity is the land itself, we handle acquisition and the plan that turns a plot into a development that can be built and sold.

Design, structure, and construction stay with one team. The same people who agree the brief stay through handover, so decisions made on paper are the decisions that get built.

How we work it in Abuja

Asokoro, Wuse, and Jahi do not share one brief. Asokoro plots are tight and the finish is judged up close. Wuse has to work with existing streets, services, and neighbours. Jahi still has room, but access, power, and drainage decide whether a scheme is livable on day one. We read each location before we lock unit sizes, parking, or the construction sequence.

Architectural and structural design are coordinated before site work starts. We model the building so clashes show up on screen, not in the slab. Site practice holds a zero tolerance on accidents. The finish is aimed at zero-defect handover, with snagging done before keys are offered.

What you receive

  • A brief that matches the plot, the access, and the demand in that part of Abuja.
  • Coordinated architectural and structural design before construction starts.
  • One team accountable from the first drawing through to handover.
  • A building you can occupy or sell, inspected against the specification rather than patched at the end.

Work on the ground

Two semi-detached blocks in Asokoro are part of this residential line: paired homes on a district where privacy, parking, and finish matter as much as floor area. Residential projects in Wuse and Jahi sit alongside that work. Wuse asks for a building that fits an established neighbourhood. Jahi asks for homes that are complete when people move in, including the services around them. All of this work is in Abuja.

From plot to keys

Bring a plot, a sketch, or a building you want taken from design to occupation. We start with the site and the numbers, then design, then build. The office is Suite E3, SHM Complex, before the Federal Ministry of Works, Mabushi. Quality you can see, reliability you can feel.
