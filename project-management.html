Services

Project Management & Consultancy

A project goes wrong long before the site looks wrong: a brief that was never tested, a cost that was guessed, a contract nobody is watching. Civil Merc stays on the job from inception to handover so those gaps are closed while they are still decisions.

What the service covers

Consultancy runs through feasibility studies, cost estimation, contract supervision, quality assurance, and risk management. Feasibility asks whether the site, the budget, and the programme can carry the brief. Cost estimation puts a figure on that brief that can be defended line by line. Contract supervision keeps the builder, the drawings, and the payments on the same job. Quality assurance checks work against the specification while it can still be corrected. Risk management names what can slip — ground, weather, approvals, supply — and assigns an owner before it becomes a delay.

How we run a job in Abuja

Abuja projects stall on approvals, access, and services as often as they stall on site. We treat local codes as part of the programme, and we apply Eurocodes and ASTM where the specification calls for them. BIM and 3D modelling keep design, quantity, and site looking at one model, so a clash is found in the office rather than in a pour.

Supervision is continuous. We do not appear only at handover. Site practice holds a zero tolerance on accidents. Quality checks are aimed at zero-defect handover: snags are closed before the client is asked to take the building. We work to at least 95% client satisfaction, measured by whether the finished job matches the brief that was agreed.

What you receive

  • A feasibility study that says what the site and the budget can support.
  • A cost estimate you can hold the contract against.
  • Supervision, quality assurance, and a risk register from inception to handover.
  • Compliance with local codes, and with Eurocodes and ASTM where they apply.

One model, one standard

When design, cost, and site each keep their own version of the job, the building that gets built is a compromise nobody approved. We use BIM and 3D modelling so the drawing, the quantity, and the work on the ground stay aligned. That is how a finish aimed at zero defects stays realistic: the defects are designed out, then checked out, rather than discovered when the client walks the floor.
